shop: architecture guide

Built from the project's own tests on 2026-11-12.

The shop turns a cart of items into an order. orders.place is the only entry point: it asks stock to reserve the items, asks pricing for the total, and asks mailer for a receipt. pricing looks every item up in catalog.

Everything is in one process and in memory. The one slow step is the price lookup in catalog, which stands in for a database call.

Architecture

One coordinator and three helpers, with one lookup at the bottom. orders coordinates and holds no business rules. stock decides whether an order is allowed. pricing owns the price and discount rules. catalog owns the price list. mailer only formats the receipt text.

How a request moves

  • orders.place(cart) receives a cart such as {"pen": 2} (orders.py:10).
  • stock.reserve runs first: check raises ValueError if any item is short, then the quantities are subtracted (stock.py:11-13).
  • An order number is taken from a counter (orders.py:12).
  • pricing.total adds up price times quantity per item, then applies the discount (pricing.py:13-14). Each item costs one call to catalog.price_of (pricing.py:5).
  • mailer.receipt turns the number and the total in cents into text such as "Order 1: 3.00 EUR" (mailer.py:2).

State and data

  • Stock lives in a module-level dictionary, STOCK (stock.py:1). It is lost when the process stops.
  • Prices live in a dictionary in catalog (catalog.py:3). The 10 ms sleep stands in for a database read (catalog.py:7).
  • Order numbers come from a counter in memory (orders.py:7), so they restart at 1 after a restart.
  • Nothing is saved to disk. There are no orders to look up afterwards.

Patterns and gotchas

  • Coordinator pattern: only orders calls the other parts. Reading place shows the whole sequence.
  • Stock is reserved before the price is worked out (orders.py:11-13). If pricing ever failed, the items would stay reserved. (inferred from the order of the calls)
  • Money is whole cents, and the discount is whole-number division (pricing.py:14), so it rounds down.
  • The discount applies when the cart holds ten or more items in total, not ten of one item (pricing.py:9).

Reading order

  • orders.place: the whole flow in four lines.
  • pricing.total and pricing.discount: the business rules.
  • stock.reserve and stock.check: when an order is refused.
  • catalog.price_of: small, but it is where the time goes, so read it before touching speed.
  • mailer.receipt: only formatting, so it can wait.
  • Run /flow small order to watch these calls happen in order.
